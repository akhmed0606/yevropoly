Our Polycarbonate Products

Multiwall Polycarbonate Sheets

Lightweight, high-insulation panel systems for thermal efficiency.

  • Structure: H-structure and X-structure available
  • Standard Production Size: 19.7 ft × 6.9 ft, or 135.7 sq ft per sheet
  • Custom Lengths: Cut-to-size lengths available. Extended project lengths can be quoted on request
  • UV Protection: Co-extruded on one side (Standard)
  • Application: Greenhouses, Roofing, Wall Systems
  • Options: Multiple thicknesses and colors available
  • Thermal Efficiency: High R-Value insulation
  • Warranty: Written 10-Year Manufacturer’s Warranty (UV Performance)
Standard UV protection is designed for long-term outdoor exposure.

Solid Polycarbonate Sheets

High impact resistance and structural durability for glazing.

  • Durability: Virtually unbreakable impact resistance
  • UV Protection: Co-extruded on both sides
  • Standard Production Size: 10 × 6.7 ft, or 67.4 sq ft per sheet
  • Custom Cutting: Cut-to-size sheets available based on project requirements
  • Warranty: Written 15-Year Manufacturer’s Warranty (UV Performance)
  • Application: Glazing, Barriers, Industrial Safety
  • Clarity: High optical clarity (Clear, Bronze, Grey)
  • Options: Multiple thicknesses and colors available
Performance varies by thickness. Refer to TDS.

Architectural Glazing Profiles & Integration

Complete your multiwall system with matching H, U, and Snap Cap profiles.

H-Profile (Standard)

Standard Joiner: Slide-in connection for joining two sheets. Allows for thermal expansion.

U-Profile (Edge)

Edge Sealing: Caps open flutes to prevent dust/moisture entry and finish the sheet edge.
